🎧 Full Extension Description & Architecture

Long reasoning chains, deep code synthesis, and research prompts often take 15 to 90 seconds to stream. Instead of staring at a blinking cursor or losing track of background tabs, AI Response Audio Scape gives you gentle auditory feedback for the exact duration of the AI response lifecycle.

  • Universal AI Platform Support: Verified and tested on ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Grok (xAI), Perplexity, DeepSeek, Microsoft Copilot, Poe, and Mistral.
  • 8-Browser Engine Certified: Engineered for Google Chrome, Brave, Comet (Perplexity), Opera / GX, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, Apple Safari (macOS & iOS), and Samsung Internet (Android).
  • Web Audio GainNode Bridge: Supports software volume slider control even on iOS Safari and mobile browsers where native HTML5 volume properties are read-only.
  • 4 Bundled Acoustic Loops: Ships with locally synthesized, loop-ready MP3 soundscapes right out of the box—no internet required.
  • Persistent Local Custom Audio: Upload personal MP3/WAV loops up to 8 MB directly into browser local storage.
  • Instant Cutoff Guarantee: Continuous heartbeat checking stops playback immediately when streaming finishes.

⚙️ Detailed Usage Instructions & Browser Guide

  • Master On/Off Switch: Toggle the switch at the top of the popup at any time to mute or resume automatic playback across all open AI tabs immediately.
  • Strict Thinking-Only Audio: Audio plays strictly when the AI is thinking, calculating, or streaming text tokens. Idle clicks, menu navigation, and prompt typing produce zero sound.
  • Adjusting Loop Volume: Drag the volume slider between 0% and 100% in the popup. Changes apply in real time via both HTML5 Audio and our Web Audio API GainNode bridge.
  • Apple Safari (macOS & iOS): Convert and run via xcrun safari-web-extension-converter or enable in Safari Settings > Extensions and grant permission for AI domains.
  • Samsung Internet & Mobile Chromium: Install via the Galaxy Store / CRX extension manager and allow site sound in permissions.
  • Firefox & Brave: Load via about:debugging in Firefox, and ensure Autoplay is permitted for your AI site in Brave Shields.

🛡️ Official Privacy Policy — 100% Local & Zero Data Collection

AI Response Audio Scape is engineered from the ground up with a strict Zero-Telemetry, 100% Local Architecture. Your conversations, prompts, AI outputs, and custom audio files never leave your computer or mobile device.

  • Zero Data Collection or Transmission: This extension does not collect, read, store, log, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, chat prompts, AI responses, IP addresses, or usage analytics.
  • Zero External Network Requests: The extension contains zero fetch(), XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket, or remote CDN calls. Every script, stylesheet, icon, and audio loop is bundled locally inside the extension package.
  • Strict Manifest V3 Content Security Policy: Enforces script-src 'self'; object-src 'self'; style-src 'self'; with zero eval(), zero inline styles, and zero remote code execution.
  • On-Device Storage Only: Sound preferences, volume levels, toggle states, and custom uploaded MP3 files are stored exclusively on your local machine via local browser storage. Uninstalling the extension permanently erases all stored preferences and custom audio data.
